A8 Chargers

Tap Add charger and pick one charger from the list; repeat for each. The list shows Bluetooth names (like “0000A8Air C0C”), not your names. Quit ISD Link and the A8 Chargers app (Mac and iPhone) first: each charger takes one connection at a time.

Each slot: charge · volts · mA · mAh · IR mΩ, then its settings. Click a slot for everything.
